OUR STORY

IT STARTED WITH THREE FRIENDS

OG Café started with three friends who shared two things —
a love for coffee and a habit of turning every coffee break into a long conversation.

One simple idea slowly became a dream: to create a place
where great coffee, good food, and great company come together.

What began with three friends and a small idea became OG Café —
a place made for conversations, celebrations, quick coffee breaks, and moments worth remembering.
